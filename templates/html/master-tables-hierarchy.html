Master Tables Hierarchy

Reference data shared across requisitions. Configured once (often synced from HCM), referenced by many entities. Level 1 domain headers are collapsible; expand further for tables and key fields.

Taxonomy levels: L1 Division → L2 Table → L3 Field group / child table → L4 Field

Compensation L1 · 4 tables

New. Grades, steps, packages, and plan definitions for pay transparency and offers.

       │                     │

compensation_grades
  • Pay bands with min / mid / max (e.g. Grade 7: $120K–$150K–$180K Annual)
compensation_steps
  • Positions within a grade, often tenure-based (e.g. Level 01: $34.41/hr)
compensation_packages
  • Named bundles of plans (e.g. Executive Package = Base + Bonus + LTI + Car)
compensation_plan_definitions
  • Catalog of plan types (e.g. Car Allowance ALLOWANCE, Annual Bonus BONUS)
